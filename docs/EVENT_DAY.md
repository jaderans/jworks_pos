# Event day checklist

## The week before

- **Events → New event** (or copy a past one). Add the booth fee when you create it.
- **Event → Plan**: work through the checklist (it comes with a JoshWorks starter list; add your own items). The Dashboard shows how much is done.
- **Event → Edit prices**: set this event's prices. Turn on *Booth-aware pricing* to see each product's floor price and a suggested price for your profit target. Base prices stay as they are.
- **Products → Bundle deals**: set up deals like *Cat Meme ×4 for ₱75*. They apply themselves at the register.
- **Production**: make sure what you're selling has a cost (by hand, recipe or batch).
- **Production → Restock plan**: pick the event; it works out how many of each to bring from what sold per hour at your last events. Buy the materials it lists, tap **Make it** on each product as you print, then **Use as stock to bring**. **Print the plan** for the studio wall.
- **Event → Stock**: check how many of each item you're bringing (the restock plan fills this in).
- **Event → Booth spend**: list everything: booth fee, tarps and cards (used up), stands (reusable, spread over events), materials. Mark what's bought and who paid.
- **Event → Team & payouts**: add who's on duty and their shifts.
- **Settings → Payment methods**: upload your GCash and Maribank QR images.
- **Settings → Email** (once, on your own phone or laptop): tap **Use Studio's mailer** (or paste the link and key from JoshWorks Studio → Settings → Email), put your email under **Send it to**, then **Send a test**.
- **Customers → Vouchers**: make the codes you'll post or hand out (e.g. KOMIKET50). **Customers → Stamp card**: turn it on if you're giving stamps.
- Every phone that will sell: open the app once online, install it, and give it its own register letter (A, B, C…).

## On the day

1. Open the app (no internet needed) and pick the event at the top.
2. **Sell**: check the event's prices on the price list next to **Open register** (event prices, bundle deals and discounts; anything without a price is flagged), then **Open register** with the float you counted.
3. **Customer screen** (if you brought a monitor or TV): plug it into the laptop, then tap **Customer screen** at the top of the register. Chrome or Edge asks once to manage windows: allow it, and the screen opens on the second monitor. Click **Full screen** there. It needs no internet. Its slides (prices, deals, rates, best sellers) make themselves; add your own posters or videos once in **Settings → Customer screen**.
4. Sell. For GCash or bank transfers, type the reference number from the customer's screen and check it arrived before handing over the items. If the event has a **prize wheel** (Event → Wheel) and the sale qualifies, the receipt shows **Spin the wheel**: tap it and the wheel spins on the customer screen. A free product prize is already rung up as a ₱0 sale; hand it over. A discount prize gives them a code for their next purchase.
5. A customer back for an order: **Find order** at the top of the register. Type the number they give (CS01, QA-07, PA-002), their name or part of their phone number, and it shows what they ordered, where it is and what's due.
6. Regulars and new fans: tap **Customer** in the cart to add them (ask before ticking "OK to message"). They earn stamps, and **Use reward** appears when one is ready. A code from a post goes in **Voucher**.
7. **Photo booth** (if a customer wants a photo sticker or a portrait drawn): **Photo booth** on Sell, then **Take the photo**. They see themselves in the frame on the customer screen. Keep it only if they say yes, then tap **Sticker printing order** or **Illustration request**, or email it to them.
8. Custom requests (a name sticker, a commission): **Orders → New order**, take a deposit, give them the order number. When it's done, **Ready**: the laptop calls the number out loud and the customer screen shows it (they also get an email, or you paste the copied message; **Call** calls it again), then **Hand over** to collect the balance.
9. Money that isn't a sale (water, tricycle, extra coins, a GCash cash-in or cash-out): **Cash in/out**. Pick **Cash** or **GCash**: only cash changes the drawer. Recorded one wrong? **Sales log** → tap it → fix or remove it (owner PIN), on the phone running that register.
10. Mistakes: **Sales** → tap the sale → *Void* (with a reason) or *Change payment*. Cashiers need the owner PIN.
11. How much is in? **Sales log → Money in**: the total and each method after cash in and out, and what the open drawers should hold.

## Closing

1. **Close register**: count each denomination. The app shows expected vs counted, over or short, and the e-wallet totals to check against your apps. On a device with email set up, the **end-of-day summary** (sales, registers, inventory, what to restock, with the PDF) is emailed to you; with no signal it goes out once there is one.
2. Save the backup file it offers (share it to Drive or Messenger when online).
3. Second phone without cloud sync: on that phone **Settings → Backup and devices → Share this device's data**; on the main phone **Merge a file**. Merging the same file twice is safe.
4. **Event → Team & payouts → Save payouts**, then mark each one paid.
5. **Event → Report**: full PDF, one-page summary for the team chat, payout slips.
6. **Event → Report → Send to Studio**, then in JoshWorks Studio: **Money → Booth events → Add to Studio**. The event becomes a job with the sales, everyone's earnings (payouts already made count as paid) and the booth costs. Studio on another device: **Download Studio file** here and **Open a POS file** there. Send it again after any change; Studio shows **Changed in the POS** and **Update** brings it in line.
7. **Mark as reported** when everything is filed.
8. Later: **Events → Compare events** to see which booths were worth it (sales per open hour and per peso of booth fee).
